# Photosynthesis

## The basic reaction

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. Using light, the cell turns carbon dioxide and water into glucose and releases oxygen as a by-product.

## Where it happens

In plants, photosynthesis takes place in chloroplasts. The green pigment chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green light, which is why leaves look green.

## Two stages

The light-dependent reactions take place in the thylakoid membranes and produce ATP and NADPH. The Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma and uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into sugars.

## RuBisCO

The enzyme RuBisCO catalyzes the first step of carbon fixation in the Calvin cycle. RuBisCO is often described as the most abundant protein on Earth.
