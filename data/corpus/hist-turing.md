# Alan Turing

## Computability

In his 1936 paper "On Computable Numbers", Alan Turing described an abstract machine that is now known as the Turing machine. He used it to show that some problems cannot be solved by any algorithm.

## Wartime work

During the Second World War Turing worked at Bletchley Park, where he helped design the Bombe, an electromechanical machine used to break messages encrypted with the Enigma machine.

## Machine intelligence

In 1950 Turing published "Computing Machinery and Intelligence", which proposed the imitation game as a test of machine intelligence. This test is now known as the Turing test.

## Recognition

The Turing Award, often called the Nobel Prize of computing, has been given by the ACM since 1966.
