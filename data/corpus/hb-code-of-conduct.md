# Code of Conduct

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from suppliers or customers only if the value is below 75 euros. Gifts above that value must be declined or reported to the compliance team.

## Conflicts of interest

Any personal relationship with a supplier, or any financial interest in a competitor, must be disclosed to your manager and to compliance.

## Speaking up

Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline, which is operated by an external provider. Lumen Freight does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
