# The Moon

## Basic facts

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It orbits Earth at an average distance of about 384,400 kilometers and completes one orbit in about 27.3 days.

## Phases

The phases of the Moon repeat every 29.5 days, which is the time from one new moon to the next. The full moon occurs when Earth is between the Sun and the Moon.

## Influence on Earth

The Moon's gravity raises tidal bulges in Earth's oceans. Over billions of years tidal friction has slowed Earth's rotation and pushed the Moon slowly away, at about 3.8 centimeters per year.

## Exploration

Apollo 11 landed the first humans on the Moon on July 20, 1969.
