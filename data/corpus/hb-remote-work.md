# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule

Employees in office-based roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared in-office day so that planning meetings happen in person.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be reachable during core hours, which run from 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.

## Home office

New hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 400 euros for a chair, desk or headset. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a vice president and are reviewed every 12 months.

## Working abroad

Employees may work from another EU country for up to 20 working days per year. Working from outside the EU is not permitted because of tax and data protection rules.
