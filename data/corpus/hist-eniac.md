# ENIAC

## Building the machine

ENIAC, the Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer, was designed by John Mauchly and J. Presper Eckert at the University of Pennsylvania. It was completed in late 1945 and publicly announced in February 1946.

## Size

ENIAC contained about 17,468 vacuum tubes and weighed about 27 tonnes. It occupied a room of roughly 170 square meters.

## Programming

ENIAC was originally programmed by setting switches and connecting cables on plugboards, which could take days for a new problem. The first programmers were six women, including Jean Jennings Bartik and Kathleen McNulty.

## Purpose

The machine was funded by the United States Army to calculate artillery firing tables.
