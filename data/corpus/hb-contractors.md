# Contractor Guidelines

## Eligibility

External contractors are not employees and are not eligible for paid time off, the pension plan or the learning budget.

## Expenses

Contractors must submit expenses within 15 days and only for costs listed in their statement of work. The hotel limit for contractors is 120 euros per night in every city.

## Access and equipment

Contractors receive a limited account that expires at the end of their contract. Contractors use their own laptops, which must have disk encryption enabled before they connect to company systems.
