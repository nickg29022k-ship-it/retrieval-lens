# Parental Leave Policy

## Leave entitlement

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The policy applies equally to birth, adoption and foster placement.

## Timing

Parental leave can be taken at any time within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be split into at most two blocks.

## Returning to work

After parental leave, employees can use a phased return of 4 weeks during which they work 80 percent of their hours at full pay.
