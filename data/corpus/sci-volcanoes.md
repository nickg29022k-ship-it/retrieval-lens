# Volcanoes

## Types of volcanoes

Shield volcanoes have gentle slopes built from fluid basaltic lava. Stratovolcanoes are steep cones built from alternating layers of lava and ash. Cinder cones are the smallest type and are built from loose fragments ejected around a single vent.

## The Ring of Fire

The Pacific Ring of Fire is a belt of subduction zones around the Pacific Ocean. It contains about 75 percent of the world's active volcanoes.

## Notable eruptions

Mount St. Helens in Washington State erupted on May 18, 1980, after a large landslide on its north flank. Mauna Loa in Hawaii is the largest active volcano on Earth by volume.

## Measuring eruptions

The Volcanic Explosivity Index ranks eruptions on a scale from 0 to 8. Each step on the scale represents roughly a tenfold increase in erupted material.
