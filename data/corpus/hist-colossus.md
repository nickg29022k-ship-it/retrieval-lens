# Colossus

## Purpose

Colossus was a series of electronic codebreaking computers built at Bletchley Park during the Second World War. Colossus was used against the Lorenz cipher used by the German high command, not against Enigma.

## Design

The first Colossus was designed by the engineer Tommy Flowers and began working in early 1944. The Mark 2 version used about 2,400 vacuum tubes.

## Secrecy

Colossus was kept secret for decades after the war, so early histories of computing often credited ENIAC as the first electronic computer. Ten Colossus machines were in use by the end of the war.
