# IT Equipment

## Laptops

Employees can choose between a MacBook Pro 14 and a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if the device fails.

## Accessories

Office-based employees receive a second monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. Drivers receive a rugged tablet for route planning instead of a laptop.

## Software

Software can be requested through the IT service desk. Installing unapproved software on company devices is not allowed.

## Returning equipment

When employees leave the company, all equipment must be returned within 10 working days.
