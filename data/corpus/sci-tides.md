# Ocean Tides

## What causes tides

Tides are the regular rise and fall of sea level caused mainly by the gravitational pull of the Moon. The Sun also produces tides, but its tidal effect is only about 46 percent as strong as the Moon's.

## Spring and neap tides

Spring tides have the largest range and occur around the new moon and the full moon, when the Sun, Moon and Earth are aligned. Neap tides have the smallest range and occur at the first and third quarter moons.

## Timing

Most coastlines experience two high tides and two low tides in a tidal day, which lasts about 24 hours and 50 minutes.

## Extreme tides

The Bay of Fundy in Canada has the largest tidal range in the world, reaching about 16 meters.
