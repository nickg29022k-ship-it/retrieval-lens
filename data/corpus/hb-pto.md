# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per month, starting from the first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Carryover

Unused PTO can be carried over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any carried-over days that are not used by March 31 are forfeited.

## Requesting time off

Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance through the HR portal. Shorter requests only need manager approval in the team calendar.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO. Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year, and a doctor's note is only required after 3 consecutive sick days.
