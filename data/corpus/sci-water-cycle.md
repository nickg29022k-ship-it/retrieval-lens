# The Water Cycle

## Overview

The water cycle describes how water moves between the oceans, the atmosphere and the land. The cycle is driven by energy from the Sun and by gravity.

## Evaporation and transpiration

Evaporation turns liquid water into water vapor. About 86 percent of global evaporation comes from the oceans. Plants also release water vapor through their leaves in a process called transpiration, and the two processes together are known as evapotranspiration.

## Condensation and precipitation

As moist air rises it cools, and water vapor condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When droplets grow heavy enough they fall as precipitation, such as rain, snow or hail. A water molecule stays in the atmosphere for about 9 days on average.

## Runoff and groundwater

Water that reaches the ground either flows over the surface as runoff or soaks into the soil and recharges groundwater aquifers.
