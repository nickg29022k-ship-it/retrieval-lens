# Lumen Freight Company Overview

## Who we are

Lumen Freight is a regional logistics company that moves containers between European ports and inland distribution centers. The company was founded in 2011 in Rotterdam by two former port logistics engineers. Today Lumen Freight employs about 1,200 people.

## Operations

Our headquarters is in Rotterdam, and we also run offices in Hamburg and Lyon. The fleet consists of 340 electric trucks and 60 diesel trucks that are being phased out by 2028. Dispatch runs around the clock from the Rotterdam operations center.

## Mission

Our mission is to make inland freight quiet, clean and predictable. Every new truck purchased since 2022 has been fully electric.
