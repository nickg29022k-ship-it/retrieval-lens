# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal and all dispatch systems.

## Devices

Laptops must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Customer data must never be copied to USB drives or personal cloud storage.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected phishing emails must be reported to the security team within 1 hour by using the Report Phish button in the mail client. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours.

## Training

All employees complete security awareness training once a year. Engineers with production access also complete a secure coding course.
