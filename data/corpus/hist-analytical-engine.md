# The Analytical Engine

## Design

The Analytical Engine was a general-purpose mechanical computer designed by Charles Babbage, who first described it in 1837. It had a separate store for memory and a mill for arithmetic, an arrangement similar to modern computers.

## Ada Lovelace

In 1843 Ada Lovelace published a translation of an article about the engine together with her own extensive notes. One of the notes contains an algorithm for computing Bernoulli numbers, which is often called the first computer program.

## Legacy

The Analytical Engine was never completed during Babbage's lifetime, mainly because of funding problems and the difficulty of manufacturing precise parts. The engine was designed to be programmed with punched cards, an idea borrowed from the Jacquard loom.
