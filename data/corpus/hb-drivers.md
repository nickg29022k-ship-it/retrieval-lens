# Driver Handbook

## Scope

This handbook covers rules that apply only to truck drivers. Where it differs from the general employee policies, the driver handbook takes precedence for drivers.

## Time off for drivers

Drivers accrue 20 days of paid time off per year because of their shift patterns. Drivers request time off through the dispatch planner at least 4 weeks before the start of the month.

## Allowances on the road

Drivers on multi-day routes receive a daily road allowance of 45 euros for food and parking. Overnight stays in approved truck stops are booked by dispatch.

## Equipment

Drivers do not receive a laptop. Each driver receives a rugged tablet, which is replaced every 2 years.

## Rest rules

Drivers must take a break of at least 45 minutes after 4.5 hours of driving, in line with EU regulations.
