# Onboarding Guide

## First day

Orientation takes place every Monday at 9:00 in the Rotterdam office, and remote hires join by video. On the first day you receive your laptop, badge and system accounts.

## Buddy program

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for the first 6 weeks. The buddy answers informal questions and introduces the new hire to colleagues.

## Required training

New employees must complete the compliance training modules within 14 days of their start date. Drivers also complete a two-day vehicle safety course before their first route.

## Check-ins

Managers hold structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. The 90-day check-in also marks the end of the probation period.
