# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting expenses

All business expenses must be submitted in the Ledgerly expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. A receipt is required for any expense above 25 euros.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 60 euros for domestic travel and 85 euros for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client dinner.

## Approval

Expenses above 500 euros require written approval from your manager before the purchase. Expenses above 2,000 euros also require approval from the finance team.

## Payment

Approved expenses are reimbursed within two pay cycles. Reimbursements are paid to the same bank account as your salary.
