# ARPANET

## Origins

ARPANET was a packet-switching network funded by the Advanced Research Projects Agency of the United States Department of Defense.

## The first message

The first message was sent on October 29, 1969, from UCLA to the Stanford Research Institute. The operator tried to type LOGIN, but the system crashed after the letters L and O were transmitted.

## TCP/IP

ARPANET switched from the older NCP protocol to TCP/IP on January 1, 1983. This date is often described as the birth of the modern Internet.

## Decommissioning

ARPANET was formally decommissioned in 1990, after its role had been taken over by NSFNET and other networks.
