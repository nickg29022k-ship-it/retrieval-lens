# Mars

## Basic facts

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. A day on Mars, called a sol, lasts 24 hours and 37 minutes. A Martian year lasts 687 Earth days.

## Moons

Mars has two small moons named Phobos and Deimos. Phobos orbits so close to the planet that it will eventually break apart or crash into the surface.

## Surface and atmosphere

Olympus Mons on Mars is the tallest volcano in the solar system, standing about 22 kilometers high. The Martian atmosphere is very thin and is made up of about 95 percent carbon dioxide.

## Exploration

The rover Perseverance landed in Jezero Crater in February 2021. It carried a small helicopter called Ingenuity, which made the first powered flight on another planet.
