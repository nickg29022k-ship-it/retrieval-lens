# UNIX

## Origins

UNIX was created at Bell Labs starting in 1969 by Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie. The first version was written in assembly language for a PDP-7 minicomputer.

## The C language

In 1973 UNIX was rewritten in the C programming language, which Dennis Ritchie had developed. This made UNIX much easier to port to new hardware.

## Design philosophy

The UNIX philosophy favors small programs that do one thing well and that can be combined with pipes. Pipes were added to UNIX in 1973.

## Descendants

Linux, macOS and the BSD family are all descended from or inspired by UNIX.
