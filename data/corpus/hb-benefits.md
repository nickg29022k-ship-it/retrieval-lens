# Employee Benefits

## Health insurance

Health insurance coverage starts on the first day of the month after your start date. The company pays 80 percent of the premium for employees and their dependents.

## Pension

Lumen Freight matches pension contributions at 100 percent up to 4 percent of base salary. Company contributions vest immediately.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Wellbeing

Employees receive a gym or sports subsidy of 50 euros per month. The employee assistance program offers free and confidential counseling sessions.
