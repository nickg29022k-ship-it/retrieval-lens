# Honeybees

## Colony structure

A honeybee colony has a single queen, thousands of female worker bees and, in summer, a few hundred male drones. The queen can lay up to 2,000 eggs per day during peak season.

## Lifespan

Worker bees born in summer live for about 5 to 6 weeks. Workers born in autumn can survive the winter and live for several months.

## The waggle dance

Foragers communicate the direction and distance of food sources with the waggle dance. The angle of the dance relative to vertical encodes the direction of the food relative to the Sun. Karl von Frisch decoded the dance and shared the 1973 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

## Honey production

Bees make honey by collecting nectar, adding enzymes and evaporating water until the sugar content is high enough to prevent spoilage.
