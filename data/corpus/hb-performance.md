# Performance Reviews

## Review cycle

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a self-assessment, peer feedback and a manager assessment.

## Ratings

Ratings use a 5-point scale, from 1 (below expectations) to 5 (exceptional). Managers meet in calibration sessions to keep ratings consistent across teams.

## Promotions

Promotions are only decided during the October cycle. A promotion case must be supported by at least two examples of work at the next level.

## Improvement plans

An employee who receives a rating of 1 is placed on a performance improvement plan that lasts 60 days.
