# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the Wayfarer travel portal at least 14 days in advance. Bookings made later need a short justification.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Train travel is preferred for any trip under 500 kilometers.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is 150 euros per night. In high-cost cities such as London, Paris and Zurich the limit is 220 euros per night.

## Travel insurance

All employees are covered by the company travel insurance policy during approved business trips.
