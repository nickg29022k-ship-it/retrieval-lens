# The Transistor

## Invention

The first working transistor, a point-contact transistor, was demonstrated at Bell Labs in December 1947 by John Bardeen and Walter Brattain. William Shockley developed the junction transistor shortly afterwards.

## Recognition

Bardeen, Brattain and Shockley shared the 1956 Nobel Prize in Physics for their work on semiconductors and the transistor effect.

## Impact

Transistors replaced vacuum tubes because they were smaller, used less power and were more reliable. Silicon later replaced germanium as the main semiconductor material, and modern processors contain billions of transistors.
